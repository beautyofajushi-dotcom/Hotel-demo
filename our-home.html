GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before you arrive.

A little planning makes for a softer landing. If something isn’t covered here, just ask.

How does a homestay at Aangan work?＋

You’ll have your own private room and plenty of space to yourself. Shared areas like the courtyard and dining room are part of the home, so you may cross paths with the family and other guests.

Can you accommodate dietary preferences?＋

Our kitchen is vegetarian, with Jain-friendly preparations possible when requested in advance. Please share any allergies or dietary needs before your visit.

Is Aangan suitable for children and older guests?＋

We welcome families. Let us know your group’s needs before booking so we can recommend the best room and discuss access, steps and arrangements.

Can you help us explore Mathura and Braj?＋

Yes. Our hosts can suggest a gentle local itinerary and help arrange a guide or transport. Exact timings and availability should be confirmed before travel.
